# Roster, approved classes and survival implementation plan

**Approval:** user selected Aegisbearer(A) and Chainbinder(B). Hexweaver/Arbalist remain unimplemented.
**Spec:** attachment eb033224-3b08-4437-842c-b0c4ce122460/Pasted text.txt; class designs docs/MILESTONE_4_ROSTER_AUDIT_AND_CLASS_PROPOSALS.md.
**Goal:** playable persistent roster/recruitment/specialisation/rescue/permadeath loop, preserving existing tactical rules outside explicit campaign survival changes.
**Architecture:** CombatController owns all effects and rescue acceptance; CombatUnit owns transient life/ward state. CampaignState owns individual records, purchases, progression and committed results. Plain version2 saves migrate actual v1 records transactionally. Existing four archetypes stay unchanged; two approved archetypes plus a separate unspecialised recruit blueprint are added.

## Contracts / decisions
- Advanced IDs: knight,ranger,mage,cleric,aegisbearer,chainbinder. Recruit blueprint ID recruit is NOT an advanced class. HeroCatalog.definitions keeps original4 for historical/default skirmish; find supports extra3. SpecialisationCatalog.ids returns6. RosterUI uses that registry.
- Persistent hero IDs separate from blueprint IDs. Config.hero_ids are blueprint IDs and may repeat in campaigns; campaign_hero_ids are2distinct persistent records. Unit.campaign_id maps back to them. hero_overrides are fresh private definitions with individual displaynames. No mutable definition state.
- NewCampaign creates4unspecialised Level1 recruits. Recruit cost12Supplies; optional basic training8Supplies raises a Level1 recruit to20XP once, allowing replacement readiness before a finale. Ordinary missionXP10+15 and thresholds0/20/50/90 unchanged. Exactly2living heroes deploy; injured may still deploy. All-dead/unfunded readiness leads to explicit campaign defeat, never a stuck screen. Costs editable in separate RosterRules Resource.
- Preserve all v1 veteranIDs/classes/levels/XP/gear/upgrades; grandfather Level1 specialists. Never reroll frozen offers or apply modifiers twice. Legacy hashes captured before edits; old attack/status defaults omitted from fingerprints. Test-only LegacyCampaignFixture preserves historical4specialist scenario coverage; new M4tests exercise real newcampaign.
- CombatUnit.survival_enabled defaultfalse; campaign configs enabletrue, standalone/tutorial preserve immediate death. Life states ACTIVE,DOWNED,STABILISED,EXTRACTED,DEAD; is_alive means active conscious (old turn/attack callers), is_surviving means notDEAD. Downed bodies don't block or act. Healing may restore downed/stabilised but neverDEAD, without immediate extra action/turn.
- Bleed-out3 future round-end checks, excluding the round of incapacitation. Stabilise stops it; additional area damage shortens remaining bleed by1 at most per action, not instant execution. Enemies don't select downed targets. Carry uses action, adjacent1.5m unobstructed, one target; movement halved, including current remaining cap; drop costs action; no physics ropes. Rescuer incapacitation drops carried ally safely.
- RescueCommand modes STABILISE,PICKUP,DROP,EXTRACT share one action. Allheroes/recruits may use them. Exit for Extraction uses existing exit; other campaign missions have a clear hero-spawn retreat zone radius2.8. Extract removes active unit (and carried ally) from combat.
- Victory recovers conscious and stabilised heroes. Draw with no remaining enemies also recovers stabilised heroes. Bleeding/unrescued heroes are lost. Failure/retreat/abandon preserves only explicitly extracted heroes (loading cancellation before battle is safe). Permanent deaths recorded once, no XP; surviving deployed actors retain participationXP. Keep old campaign result flags and add explicit survival outcomes. Newrules clearly explained in briefing/UI/debrief.
- Aegis ward3/4m/CD2, two future target turns; ShieldBash2melee+Slow/CD2. ChainReel2damage,pull2m,range5/CD2; Tow friendly/downed2m,range4/CD3. Displacement static/world+livingbody clearance, no throughwalls, spend only on useful legal route; bounded collision-aware execution. No new actions, no free actor movement, camera independent.
