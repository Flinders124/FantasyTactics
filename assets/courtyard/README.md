# Overgrown ruined courtyard

Milestone 5 extends this kit to three presets. See [current environment/objective setup](../../OBJECTIVES.md). Nine additional original native meshes are generated by `source/courtyard_detail_source.gd` and `res://tests/build_courtyard_details.gd`: fountain, damaged guardian statue, recessed facade, bench, barrel, crate, broken cart, ground cracks and leaf litter. `details_manifest.json` records exact triangle counts (14–384 each) and classification. Fountain/statue collision resources use their visible triangles. Their first risers exceed navigation climb height, preventing false walkable stairs; all gameplay blockers are finalized before baking.

Exterior prop clusters remain beyond the inaccessible boundary and have no physics. Repeated flush ground marks are below targeting graphics; small plants have no collision. `detail_density` (0–2, default 1) complements the existing cosmetic densities without changing layout. `materials/sway.gdshader` / `sway.tres` animate opaque vegetation with a 0.025 m maximum sway, no particles or dynamic lights. Tall new scenery uses the same reversible fading, including camera-to-objective checks. All new art is original and has no third-party attribution or runtime dependency. The historical kit notes below describe the original 16 assets; the current kit has 25 meshes and five exported blocker colliders.

Original native Godot mesh kit, created for Fantasy Tactics on 21 October 2026. No downloaded models, textures, fonts or other third-party environment assets. All materials use vertex colours, matte surfaces and restrained greens; no texture imports or external modelling runtime are required. Compatible with the project's Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility renderer.

## Editable sources and outputs

- `source/courtyard_mesh_source.gd`: editable SurfaceTool mesh construction, bevelled/chipped masonry, lofted pillars and rocks, arch voussoirs, foliage, ivy, moss and paving. These are actual saved triangle meshes, not renamed primitive nodes.
- `../../tests/build_courtyard_assets.gd`: exports the 16 native `meshes/*.res` assets, three `collision/*.res` resources, shared stone/foliage materials and `manifest.json`. Earth material is authored directly in `materials/earth.tres`.
- `../../scripts/courtyard_kit.gd`: sized blocking props, low boundary stones and ground collision.
- `../../scripts/courtyard_dressing.gd`: separate cosmetic RNG, MultiMesh batches and exterior landmarks.
- `../../scripts/courtyard_atmosphere.gd`: daylight, procedural sky, ambient fill, standard distance fog and directional shadows.
- `../../scripts/courtyard_occlusion.gd`: scoped, reversible foreground fading.

Rebuild from the project root:

```powershell
Godot_console.exe --headless --path . --script res://tests/build_courtyard_assets.gd
```

The generated native resources ship with the project; rebuilding is optional. Modelling source remains editable without Blender. `manifest.json` records exact bounds, surface counts and triangle counts. Each asset has one material surface: wall 3,114 triangles; ruined plinth 2,688; pillar 140; arch 736; rubble 288; shrub 784; grass 27; fern 77; ivy 44; moss 12; boundary stone 64; each of five paving variants 32. Stone and foliage materials are shared, roughness 0.95, vertex colour interpreted as sRGB. Leaves are opaque and double-sided, with no alpha-cutout textures. Only temporary occluder fading uses transparency.

## Gameplay geometry contract

The original seeded obstacle centres, dimensions, separated spawn zones, 1.5 m clearance, bounded placement attempts, retries and fallback are preserved. Long footprints select broken walls; narrow tall footprints select pillars; other footprints select ruined plinths. Vertical origins sit on ground; long walls rotate to match their footprint. Sized collision faces are baked into unscaled static collision shapes.

- Walls use a continuous jagged wall shell collider; facing masonry protrudes only about 1 mm in normalized coordinates. Pillars and plinths use their actual triangle mesh collision, including bevels and broken crowns. Plinth facing protrusion is at most 0.2% of footprint width. The footprint is the conservative placement envelope; navigation is baked from final static collision, so visual contour changes can alter exact paths without altering seeded placement.
- Blocking bodies use physics mask **9**: world/movement bit 1 plus static line-of-sight bit 8. Existing combat queries therefore see the same obstruction as movement. Ground uses mask **11**, adding ground picking bit 2. Units retain existing masks.
- NavigationRegion parses static colliders, after all gameplay geometry is finalized. Existing synchronous bake and navigation-map/region synchronization finish before spawn clearance/connectivity validation and combat setup. Agent radius remains 0.5 m; unit collision and command rules are unchanged.
- Dressing is a **sibling** of NavigationRegion, has no collision bodies or navigation layers, and never participates in baking, picking or attack obstruction. Exterior arches/walls/rocks remain beyond the non-traversable boundary. Grass on boundary stones lies outside the walkable footprint. Spawn areas stay clear; combat corridors receive only flush paving and flat moss.
- Logical ground stays y=0. Paving tops are y=0.018 m, moss y=0.021 m, below unit foot origins (0.025 m), target rings (0.06 m) and path strips (0.07 m). This small visual offset prevents z-fighting without terrain elevation or gameplay steps.
- Exterior ground extends beyond the camera's view range, preventing visible skirt corners during normal rotation. Boundary kerbs clearly separate playable space from decorative ruins.

Cosmetic seed is `(original_seed XOR 0x4355525459415244 XOR attempt*104729 XOR dressing_seed_offset)`, masked positive. All paving, foliage, rubble and exterior ruin transforms use that dedicated generator. Cosmetic settings never consume gameplay RNG. `vegetation_density` and `rubble_density` default to 1 (range 0–2); `dressing_seed_offset` defaults to 0. Retry reconstructs identical dressing from the cached layout, seed and settings. Changing cosmetic settings intentionally changes dressing only.

## Visibility, lighting and performance

Daylight uses a warm directional sun, cool ambient fill, procedural sky and density 0.002 distance fog. No SSAO, volumetric fog, bloom or transparent foliage dependency. Outside ruins are height-limited and concentrated beyond boundaries. The visibility controller samples every 0.08 seconds and fades prop AABBs intersecting camera-to-active-unit, hovered target or selected destination segments. Opacity defaults to 0.24; shadows are suppressed only while faded. Shared materials, physics and navigation are never modified. Original material overrides and shadow settings restore on a clear view, result, reset, shutdown and scene exit. AABB tests are deliberately conservative, so occasionally an adjacent ruin fades too.

Paving, grass, ferns, shrubs, moss, rubble and boundary courses use MultiMesh instancing. Larger landmarks and blocking props share mesh/material resources. No static GDScript resource caches retain the kit after encounter disposal.

## Showcase and verification

Open `scenes/courtyard_showcase.tscn` and press F6: real generated seed **7**, Knight/Mage, AI disabled for review. RMB orbit, MMB/WASD pan, wheel zoom, F focus active. **F8** rotates a quarter turn; **F9** toggles the normal combat HUD. F5 retains the normal title/party/combat flow. The old direct `combat_encounter.tscn` development fixture intentionally retains its fixed greybox collision tests; all configured/main-flow encounters use the courtyard.

Run `tests/run_regressions.ps1 -Godot C:/path/to/Godot_console.exe` for automated checks. `tests/courtyard_tests.gd` checks cosmetic determinism/isolation and visibility restoration. `tests/courtyard_review.gd` runs rendered captures of seeds 7, 19 and 23 at three rotations and both target resolutions; it also captures shared movement/Fireburst previews and a short uncapped rendering sample. It requires a graphics display. Existing `tests/stability_visual.gd` exercises real input, complete generated encounters and result/replay screens. See `../../../verification/COURTYARD.md` for actual results and limitations.

The exterior is intentionally a sparse ruin garden on flat earth. There are no trees, distant terrain meshes, animated vegetation, audio or new mechanics. Very close zoom exposes the stylized flat-colour treatment; the kit is tuned for tactical viewing.
