# Revision 2: follow the score

The original had a regular 30 fps cadence, but the camp source ended before its slot, party cards held still, and action clips resolved early then held. Its end-card poster also replaced frame zero with a different shot. The revision addresses the source and the edit rather than merely interpolating the old film.

- Capture and deliver at native 60 fps; no invented interpolated frames.
- Every shot has real continuous camera travel. Camera impact shake is disabled only in these capture fixtures.
- The actual 80 BPM score (detected by Hyperframes and confirmed by its authored source) sets the 0.75 s beat / 3 s bar grid. Major scene changes land at 3, 6, 9 seconds; character selection lands at 7.5 seconds near the existing 7.65-second click. Action impacts stay aligned with the preserved cues at 11 and 13.5 seconds, and the final reveal follows the original victory sting at 16 seconds.
- Full-width moving scenes replace the former static split-screen sequence. A descending combat camera leads into close action, rather than parking over the battlefield. Single-pass brass seams bridge scene cuts.
- Keep the complete original music/SFX mix. The final mux stream-copies the original AAC packets for bit-identical audio.
- The opening is designed as a settled poster from frame zero, eliminating the original one-frame end-card flash.
- Preserve the first version as brag-v1.mp4 and brag-v1.jpg.

All footage still comes from the game: source art-direction stage, real headquarters, existing selected-hero UI snippets, and the existing developer combat fixture with authoritative commands. No production game code changes.
